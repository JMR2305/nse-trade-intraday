# Phase 6 – Strategy Evolution Laboratory

IMPORTANT

This phase is research-only.

Do NOT:
- Place live orders.
- Modify existing Trade Decision logic.
- Modify paper-trading execution.
- Automatically change strategy parameters.
- Automatically promote any strategy into production.
- Introduce look-ahead bias.

All new capabilities are for controlled research and require explicit human approval before any future deployment.

==========================================================
OBJECTIVE
==========================================================

Build a Strategy Evolution Laboratory that allows the platform to generate, compare, test and rank new strategy variations using the existing Research Factory.

Instead of asking:

"Did this strategy work?"

the platform should answer:

"How can this strategy be improved while remaining statistically robust?"

4. ROBUSTNESS TESTING
==========================================================

Automatically reject strategies that:

Depend on very few trades.

Depend on one market regime.

Depend on one sector.

Depend on one stock.

Lose after realistic costs.

Have unstable walk-forward results.

Show probable overfitting.

Have inconsistent calibration.

Create a Robustness Score.

==========================================================
8. SURVIVAL RULES
==========================================================

A strategy survives only if it:

Improves Profit Factor.

Maintains or improves Sharpe.

Does not materially increase drawdown.

Maintains calibration.

Passes walk-forward validation.

Passes evidence thresholds.

Otherwise archive it.
