- Win rate
- Average return
- Profit factor
- Expectancy
- Max drawdown
- Reliability score

Then generate strategy weights:

If strategy win rate > 60% and profit factor > 1.5:
Increase weight

If win rate < 45% or profit factor < 1:
Decrease weight

If trades < 10:
Mark as “Not enough data”
