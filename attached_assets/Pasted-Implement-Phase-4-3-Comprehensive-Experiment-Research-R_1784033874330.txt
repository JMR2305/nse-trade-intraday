Implement Phase 4.3 — Comprehensive Experiment Research Report Engine.

IMPORTANT SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

This phase is strictly for research and paper trading.

Do not:
- Place real orders.
- Connect this feature to live order execution.
- Modify live paper-trading decisions.
- Change the current Trade Decisions, Market Scanner, AI Decision, portfolio manager, ranking, confidence, or strategy-selection logic.
- Automatically promote any experiment or strategy.
- overwrite existing experiment results.
- alter the existing walk-forward calculation methodology.
- introduce lookahead bias.

All analysis must use only information that was available within each experiment’s valid train/test boundaries.

Every new UI section must clearly display:

“Research only — out-of-sample historical performance does not guarantee future results. This report does not affect live or paper-trading strategy selection.”

==================================================
PHASE 4.3 OBJECTIVE
==================================================

After every Research Factory experiment finishes, automatically generate a detailed and structured research report explaining:

1. What happened.
2. Why the result occurred.
3. Where the strategy performed well.
4. Where it failed.
5. Whether the evidence is reliable.
6. Which improvements are supported by evidence.
7. What experiment should be run next.
8. Whether the strategy should be rejected, investigated further, or remain inconclusive.

The report must be available:

- Inside the Research Factory experiment detail page.
- As downloadable JSON.
- As downloadable CSV files.
- As a printable HTML report.
- Through a stable backend API.
- In the complete experiment export.

Do not generate unsupported conclusions. Use deterministic calculations and rule-based research summaries. An LLM-generated narrative may be added only as an optional explanatory layer, but all claims must be traceable to computed metrics.

==================================================
19. FINAL RESEARCH VERDICT
==================================================

The final verdict must be one of:

- REJECT
- INSUFFICIENT DATA
- INCONCLUSIVE
- CONTINUE RESEARCH
- PROMISING — RESEARCH ONLY

Do not use APPROVED, DEPLOY, LIVE, SAFE FOR CAPITAL, or similar language.

Suggested minimum logic:

REJECT when:
- Net expectancy is materially negative with sufficient evidence, or
- Profit factor is materially below 1 with sufficient evidence, or
- Multiple robustness failures exist.

INSUFFICIENT DATA when:
- Too few OOS trades, or
- Too few windows, or
- Missing core evidence.

INCONCLUSIVE when:
- Metrics are close to zero or inconsistent across windows.

CONTINUE RESEARCH when:
- Some evidence is positive but robustness requirements are not met.

PROMISING — RESEARCH ONLY when:
- Positive net expectancy.
- Profit factor above configured threshold.
- Acceptable drawdown.
- Multiple profitable windows.
- Minimum trade count met.
- No severe concentration.
- No hard overfitting flags.

Even “PROMISING — RESEARCH ONLY” must not affect the live system.

Display the exact reasons and thresholds used.
