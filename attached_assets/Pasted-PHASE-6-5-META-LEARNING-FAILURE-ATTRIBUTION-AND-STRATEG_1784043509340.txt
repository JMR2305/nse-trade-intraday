PHASE 6.5 — META-LEARNING, FAILURE ATTRIBUTION, AND STRATEGY-GATING INTELLIGENCE

Objective:
Upgrade the existing Research Intelligence and Strategy Evolution modules so the platform learns WHY a strategy succeeds or fails, under which market conditions it should be enabled, and what measurable conditions would justify re-testing it.

This phase is strictly RESEARCH ONLY.

Do not:
- modify live or paper-trading decisions
- place orders
- auto-promote any strategy
- change the current production strategy-selection logic
- use future or test-period information during training
- introduce look-ahead leakage

Preserve all existing Research Factory, Walk-Forward Validation, Research Intelligence, Strategy Evolution, CSV, JSON, and HTML export functionality.

==================================================

==================================================

Create a backend service that analyses completed out-of-sample trades and experiment results across:

- strategy
- strategy version
- market regime
- sector
- stock
- confidence band
- holding period
- entry subtype
- exit reason
- ADX band
- ATR percentile
- volatility regime
- volume ratio
- opening gap
- EMA alignment
- trend direction
- RSI band
- MACD histogram state
- time period
- experiment window

The engine must identify conditions associated with:

- positive expectancy
- negative expectancy
- high drawdown
- poor calibration
- false-positive trades
- premature exits
- excessive holding periods
- profit concentration
- few-trade dependence
- regime-specific success or failure
- sector-specific success or failure

Only use data available before each out-of-sample decision.

==================================================
2. FAILURE ATTRIBUTION
==================================================

For every strategy and strategy version, generate a Failure Attribution report.

Include:

- primary failure reason
- secondary contributing reasons
- evidence strength
- sample size
- expectancy per trade
- profit factor
- win rate
- maximum drawdown
- calibration error
- confidence-band performance
- affected market regimes
- affected sectors
- affected holding periods
- top losing conditions
- top profitable conditions
- whether costs caused the failure
- whether the strategy had negative gross edge before costs
- whether failure was broad or concentrated

Example classifications:

- NEGATIVE_GROSS_EDGE
- COST_SENSITIVE
- REGIME_MISMATCH
- POOR_ENTRY_FILTER
- POOR_EXIT_LOGIC
- OVERCONFIDENT
- FEW_TRADE_DEPENDENT
- SECTOR_CONCENTRATION
- STOCK_CONCENTRATION
- EXCESSIVE_DRAWDOWN
- UNSTABLE_ACROSS_WINDOWS
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
- POSSIBLE_EDGE_REQUIRES_MORE_DATA

Do not present causal claims unless supported by evidence. Use wording such as “associated with” when causality is not established.

==================================================
3. CONDITION-BASED STRATEGY ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

For each strategy, generate a research-only eligibility policy.

Example:

Strategy: Trend Rider

Eligible only when:
- ADX >= 22
- ATR percentile between 35 and 80
- price above EMA20 and EMA50
- market regime is Bullish or Neutral-Bullish
- volume ratio >= 0.80
- calibrated confidence >= 65
- minimum historical sample requirement is met

Ineligible when:
- ADX < 18
- sideways regime detected
- negative rolling expectancy
- profit factor below 1.0
- confidence calibration is unreliable
- insufficient evidence exists

Each rule must display:

- condition
- threshold
- source metric
- sample size
- expectancy with condition
- expectancy without condition
- uplift
- confidence level
- whether the rule passed robustness checks

Do not activate these rules in the live system. Label them:

RESEARCH CANDIDATE — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED

==================================================
4. ROBUSTNESS AND ANTI-OVERFITTING RULES
==================================================

A meta-learning insight must not be treated as reliable unless it satisfies configurable checks such as:

- minimum 30 out-of-sample trades
- minimum 2 test windows
- positive expectancy in more than one window
- acceptable drawdown
- no severe stock concentration
- no severe sector concentration
- no single-month dependence
- no top-five-trade dependence
- survives reasonable parameter perturbation
- survives cost and slippage stress
- no look-ahead leakage
- no future-data contamination

Evidence labels:

- INSUFFICIENT
- VERY LOW
- LOW
- MODERATE
- STRONG

Use conservative thresholds. Never label small samples as strong evidence.

5. ADD A NEW “META-LEARNING” TAB
==================================================

Add a new tab inside Research Intelligence named:

Meta-Learning

Sections:

A. Strategy Health Summary
Show each strategy with:

- overall status
- OOS trades
- expectancy
- profit factor
- drawdown
- evidence strength
- dominant failure reason
- best regime
- worst regime
- recommended research action

Possible research actions:

- KEEP RESEARCHING
- RESTRICT BY REGIME
- MODIFY ENTRY FILTER
- MODIFY EXIT LOGIC
- REDUCE HOLDING PERIOD
- REQUIRE MORE DATA
- ARCHIVE
- REJECT
- PROMISING — HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

B. Why Strategies Failed
Expandable cards containing:

- failure attribution
- supporting metrics
- affected conditions
- gross versus net performance
- reliability warnings

C. Eligibility Map
A matrix:

Rows:
- strategies

Columns:
- Bullish
- Bearish
- Neutral-Bullish
- Neutral-Bearish
- Sideways
- High Volatility
- Low Volatility

Cells should show:

- eligible
- research-only
- insufficient evidence
- ineligible
- sample count
- expectancy
- profit factor

D. What Would Improve the Strategy
Display measurable changes that could justify another experiment.

Examples:

- require ADX >= 22
- remove entries after gaps above 2%
- shorten max holding from 20 to 10 days
- avoid Neutral-Bearish regimes
- require volume ratio >= 0.8
- use ATR-based exit
- raise or lower confidence threshold

Every suggestion must show its historical uplift and sample size.

E. Contradictions
Detect and display contradictions such as:

- high confidence but negative expectancy
- research intelligence says profitable while walk-forward validation says unprofitable
- strategy marked enabled despite negative rolling edge
- strategy performs well overall but fails in the current regime
- a child strategy is worse than its parent
- reported positive return caused by only a few trades

6. STRATEGY EVOLUTION INTEGRATION
==================================================

Connect Meta-Learning to Strategy Evolution.

Allow Meta-Learning findings to create DRAFT research mutations such as:

- regime restriction
- ADX threshold
- ATR percentile threshold
- volume confirmation
- gap filter
- confidence threshold
- holding-period adjustment
- exit-rule adjustment
- sector restriction

Rules:

- mutations must remain DRAFT
- no automatic promotion
- human approval required before testing
- each mutation must include its parent strategy
- include the evidence that generated it
- include expected benefit
- include known risk
- include sample size
- include experiment hypothesis
- include an undo/archive action

Do not create unlimited mutations. Limit suggestions to the top 3 evidence-supported changes per strategy.
