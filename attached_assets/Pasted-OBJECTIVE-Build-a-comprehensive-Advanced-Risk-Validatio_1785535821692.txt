OBJECTIVE

Build a comprehensive Advanced Risk Validation Framework for ApexQuant AI.

This module continuously validates portfolio risk, strategy risk, execution risk and market risk using independent cross-checks.

This module MUST remain completely READ-ONLY and ADVISORY-ONLY.

It MUST NEVER

• Modify portfolio

• Modify positions

• Modify AI

• Modify strategies

• Change limits

• Place orders

• Execute trades

Its responsibility is to validate, score, stress-test and explain risk.
