11. EFFECTIVE-TIME SAFETY
==================================================

Changing the universe must NOT silently alter an already-running NSE session.

Default rule:

If activation is approved while market is open:
effective_from = NEXT_TRADING_SESSION

If approved before the session cutoff:
it may become effective for the next eligible natural session.

Do not swap active universe in the middle of:

- Phase 5A collection
- Phase 5B/5C lifecycle
- canonical scan
- strategy evaluation
- order decision
- open-position management
