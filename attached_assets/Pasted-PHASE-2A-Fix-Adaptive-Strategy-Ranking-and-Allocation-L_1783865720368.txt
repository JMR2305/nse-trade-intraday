PHASE 2A — Fix Adaptive Strategy Ranking and Allocation Logic

The adaptive strategy-selection feature is implemented, but the latest walk-forward result became worse:

- Previous full-model return: approximately -1.29%
- Current adaptive-model return: approximately -3.31%
- Current profit factor: 0.71
- Current expectancy: approximately -₹57 per trade
- Trades reduced from 48 to 29

The Strategy Intelligence section reveals a ranking defect:

- supertrend_follow is ENABLED even though its rolling profit factor is only 0.35 and rolling expectancy is negative.
- mean_reversion has rolling profit factor around 1.80 and positive expectancy, but receives the same allocation.
- The system appears to re-enable a losing strategy solely to satisfy a minimum diversification rule.

Fix the ranking and allocation policy. Do not add new indicators or strategies in this task.

1. Remove forced diversification into losing strategies

Do not enable a strategy merely because the system requires a minimum number of strategies.

If only one strategy has a demonstrated positive edge, allow only one strategy to be enabled.

Cash is preferable to allocating capital to a losing strategy.

Remove any rule such as:
- always enable at least two strategies
- re-enable the highest-ranked disabled strategy for diversification
- force full capital deployment

2. Add hard eligibility gates

A strategy-regime combination may receive capital only when all of these conditions pass:

- Minimum completed training trades: configurable, default 30
- Rolling profit factor >= 1.10
- Rolling expectancy after costs > 0
- Rolling net return after costs > 0
- Maximum drawdown <= configured limit
- Data quality valid
- No look-ahead leakage
- Strategy is not unstable across recent windows

Preferred stronger eligibility:

- Profit factor >= 1.20
- Positive expectancy
- At least 50 completed trades
- Positive results in at least two independent historical periods or windows

If no strategy passes, hold cash and issue no BUY recommendation.

3. Correct the ranking formula

Rank only strategies that pass the eligibility gates.

Use a transparent composite score based on out-of-sample or prior completed trades:

- 35% normalized profit factor
- 30% normalized expectancy after costs
- 15% normalized Sharpe or downside-risk-adjusted return
- 10% drawdown score
- 10% sample-size and stability score

Do not allow win rate alone to dominate ranking.

Cap extreme values so one noisy metric cannot overwhelm the score.

Display every score component in Strategy Intelligence.

4. Apply uncertainty shrinkage

Small samples must be shrunk toward neutral performance.

Examples:

- A strategy with PF 2.0 over 5 trades must not outrank PF 1.3 over 150 trades solely because of the higher raw PF.
- Use Bayesian shrinkage, confidence intervals, or another deterministic sample-size adjustment.
- Show raw PF, adjusted PF, raw expectancy, adjusted expectancy, and sample count.

5. Use regime-specific evidence correctly

Priority:

1. Strategy + current regime, when sufficiently sampled
2. Strategy across related regimes
3. Global strategy history

Do not use a poorly sampled exact regime when a broader, more reliable estimate is available.

Show:
- Evidence level used
- Sample count
- Fallback reason
- Reliability classification

6. Correct capital allocation

Allocate only among eligible strategies.

Allocation must be proportional to positive adjusted edge, subject to:

- Maximum 40% per strategy by default
- Maximum 30% per sector
- Maximum portfolio risk limits
- Position-size limits
- Available capital

Do not split equally unless adjusted edges are effectively equal.

Unallocated capital must remain as cash.

Display:
- Eligible allocation
- Final allocation
- Capital held as cash
- Reason for any cap or reduction

7. Add explicit status categories

Use:

- ENABLED
- WATCHLIST
- DISABLED_NEGATIVE_EDGE
- DISABLED_INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE
- DISABLED_UNSTABLE
- DISABLED_REGIME_MISMATCH
- CASH_ONLY

Do not label a negative-PF strategy as ENABLED.

8. Add walk-forward comparison variants

Run all variants on identical unseen data:

A. Base technical engine  
B. Calibrated full model before adaptive ranking  
C. Existing adaptive ranking logic  
D. Corrected gated ranking and edge-weighted allocation  
E. Cash when no eligible strategy exists  

Show:

- Net return after all costs
- Net profit
- Trades
- Win rate
- Profit factor
- Expectancy
- Sharpe
- Maximum drawdown
- Exposure
- Turnover
- Total costs
- Percentage of time held in cash

Do not automatically deploy variant D unless it improves out-of-sample quality and passes safeguards.

9. Add diagnostic table for rejected trades

For every trade rejected by the new eligibility rules, show:

- Stock
- Strategy
- Regime
- Proposed date
- Rejection reason
- PF
- Expectancy
- Sample count
- What the trade outcome would have been

This is analysis-only and must not introduce look-ahead into decisions.

10. Add automated tests

Test:

- A strategy with PF below 1.0 cannot be enabled
- A strategy cannot be re-enabled only for diversification
- Cash is allowed when no strategy qualifies
- Positive-edge strategies receive more allocation than weaker ones
- Small-sample extreme performance is shrunk
- Allocation caps work
- Sector caps work
- Ranking is deterministic
- Only prior completed trades are used
- Same-window outcomes never affect selection
- Costs are included in expectancy and PF
- No fallback/mock data is used for learning
- Previous behavior remains reproducible as a comparison variant

Run using the same configuration as the previous walk-forward test so results are directly comparable.

   - Base technical model
