PHASE 20 — AUTOMATIC MARKET-HOURS SCANNING, PAPER-TRADE EXECUTION, AND END-TO-END VALIDATION

Goal:
Turn the currently connected Zerodha-based research platform into a dependable paper-trading system that automatically scans fresh market data, creates simulated trades only when all quality and risk gates pass, manages open paper positions, records outcomes, and proves that every decision is reproducible and auditable.

The system must remain PAPER TRADING / RESEARCH ONLY.
LIVE ORDER PLACEMENT, MODIFICATION, AND CANCELLATION MUST REMAIN DISABLED.

CURRENT STATE

- Published production app is live.
- Zerodha Kite Connect is authenticated and valid.
- Live quotes are available.
- Global freshness timestamps and scan IDs are visible across pages.
- Stale, fallback, failed, or mismatched data blocks BUY recommendations.
- Paper trading is active.
- Live orders are disabled.

PART 1 — AUTOMATIC SCAN SCHEDULER

Implement a production-safe automatic scanner.

Requirements:

1. Run only during NSE market hours using Asia/Kolkata time.
2. Default interval: every 5 minutes.
3. Make interval configurable from Settings:
   - 1 minute
   - 3 minutes
   - 5 minutes
   - 10 minutes
   - 15 minutes
4. Do not run on weekends, NSE holidays, or outside market hours.
5. Use a distributed lease/lock so Replit Autoscale instances cannot start duplicate scans.
6. Recover automatically from expired or abandoned locks.
7. Preserve the last successful snapshot if a scan fails.
8. Never mark failed data as fresh.
9. Record:
   - scan_id
   - started_at
   - completed_at
   - duration
   - trigger source: SCHEDULED or MANUAL
   - symbols requested
   - symbols received
   - missing symbols
   - stale symbols
   - unavailable symbols
   - data provider
   - result status
   - error details
10. Add scheduler health:
   - last scheduled attempt
   - last successful scheduled scan
   - next scheduled scan
   - missed-run count
   - lock owner/lease expiry
   - scheduler status

PART 2 — PAPER-TRADE ELIGIBILITY GATES

A paper BUY may be created only when all mandatory gates pass:

- scan status is FRESH
- snapshot consistency is PASS
- quote provider is Zerodha
- no fallback data
- symbol quote is available and current
- market is open
- strategy is eligible for the current regime
- recommendation is BUY or STRONG BUY
- confidence meets configured threshold
- opportunity score meets configured threshold
- trade-quality score meets configured threshold
- minimum risk/reward is met
- stop-loss is valid
- position-size calculation succeeds
- sufficient simulated cash exists
- per-stock exposure cap passes
- sector exposure cap passes
- portfolio risk cap passes
- daily trade-count limit passes
- cooldown and duplicate-position checks pass

If any gate fails:
- do not create the paper trade
- record the exact failed gates
- show the reason in AI Decision, Trade Decisions, Signals, and Notifications

PART 3 — PAPER ENTRY EXECUTION

When all gates pass:

1. Create a simulated order and position.
2. Use the latest eligible quote as the fill basis.
3. Support configurable paper fill models:
   - LAST_TRADED_PRICE
   - NEXT_QUOTE
   - SLIPPAGE_ADJUSTED
4. Default to SLIPPAGE_ADJUSTED.
5. Add configurable slippage and estimated charges.
6. Persist:
   - trade_id
   - scan_id
   - symbol
   - strategy
   - side
   - signal timestamp
   - decision timestamp
   - simulated order timestamp
   - fill timestamp
   - signal price
   - fill price
   - quantity
   - stop
   - target
   - risk amount
   - estimated charges
   - slippage
   - confidence
   - opportunity score
   - trade-quality score
   - regime
   - sector
   - full decision evidence

Do not create duplicate entries for the same symbol while an open position exists.

PART 4 — PAPER POSITION MANAGEMENT

On each successful scheduled scan, evaluate open paper positions.

Support exits for:

- stop-loss hit
- target hit
- bearish/EXIT recommendation
- trailing stop
- time-based exit
- market-close exit when configured
- maximum holding period
- portfolio risk reduction
- sector cap breach
- stale or unavailable data safety exit recommendation

Important:
- Stale data must not fabricate an exit fill.
- If a reliable quote is unavailable, mark the exit as PENDING_DATA and notify the user.
- Record the rule that triggered each exit.

PART 6 — NO LOOK-AHEAD AND REPRODUCIBILITY

Ensure:

1. Every paper trade is linked to the exact scan_id and snapshot_ts used.
2. No future candle, quote, result, or revised data is used.
3. Replaying a trade with the same stored snapshot and configuration produces the same decision.
4. Store strategy version, model version, rule version, and configuration hash with every trade.
5. Historical corrections must not silently rewrite past decisions.
6. Any recomputation must be labelled RECOMPUTED and preserve the original record.
