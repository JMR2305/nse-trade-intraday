OBJECTIVE

Build a comprehensive Operational Control Centre for ApexQuant AI.

This module becomes the primary operational dashboard for monitoring and managing the platform during market hours.

This module MUST remain completely READ-ONLY and ADVISORY-ONLY.

It MUST NEVER

• Place orders

• Execute trades

• Modify portfolio

• Modify strategies

• Modify AI models

• Change risk parameters

• Modify feature flags automatically

• Restart services automatically

The module should monitor, coordinate and advise only.
