Build Version 2.4 — Walk-Forward Validation and Realistic Execution Simulator.

Goal:
Test the complete Trade Decision Engine on unseen historical periods using realistic execution assumptions.

Important:
- Paper trading and research only.
- Do not modify existing strategy rules, confidence rules, learning logic, similarity logic, or portfolio rules during a validation run.
- Prevent lookahead bias and data leakage.
- Do not use mock or synthetic data.
- Validation results must be reproducible.

1. Create backend modules

Create:

backend/walk_forward_validator.py
backend/execution_simulator.py
backend/validation_metrics.py

2. Walk-forward structure

Support:

Training window:
- 1 year
- 2 years
- 3 years

Testing window:
- 1 month
- 3 months
- 6 months

Step size:
- 1 month
- 3 months

Example:

Train:
January 2022 to December 2023

Test:
January 2024 to March 2024

Then move forward:

Train:
April 2022 to March 2024

Test:
April 2024 to June 2024

Repeat until the available dataset ends.

3. Strict data separation

During every testing window:

- Historical Knowledge must contain only data available before the test date.
- Similarity search must not use trades from the current or future testing period.
- Adaptive learning must use only completed trades from earlier periods.
- Pattern Quality must be calculated only from training data.
- No future candle may influence entry, exit, indicators, confidence, regime, or ranking.
- Log the maximum data timestamp used for every decision.

4. Test the complete decision pipeline

For each historical trading day:

1. Reconstruct the market using data available at that time.
2. Run the Market Scanner.
3. Run Trade Decisions.
4. Apply historical pattern adjustment.
5. Apply adaptive learning adjustment.
6. Apply similarity adjustment.
7. Apply hard risk filters.
8. Run Portfolio Manager.
9. Record recommendations.
10. Simulate the resulting trades.

Do not use the final outcome when generating the original recommendation.

13. Validation verdict

Return one of:

- PASSED
- PASSED WITH CAUTION
- FAILED
- INSUFFICIENT DATA

Suggested PASSED requirements:

- Positive out-of-sample expectancy
- Profit factor above 1.15 after costs
- Maximum drawdown within configured limit
- Full model outperforms base model
- Results are not dependent on only one stock, sector, or test window
- At least 100 completed out-of-sample trades

Do not hardcode these requirements; make them configurable and visible.
