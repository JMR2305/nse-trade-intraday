====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Build a validation and analytics layer that measures how well the Phase 5A Pre-Open Intelligence module predicts actual post-open market behaviour.

The system must compare:

- pre-open indicative price
- final pre-open price
- actual opening price
- price at 09:20
- price at 09:30
- price at 10:00
- price at 10:30
- intraday high
- intraday low
- market close

The purpose is to determine whether:

- high-ranked candidates outperform low-ranked candidates
- buy imbalance predicts upward continuation
- sell imbalance predicts downward continuation
- gap-up stocks continue or reverse
- sector confirmation improves prediction quality
- executed pre-open quantity improves signal reliability

====================================================
PART 2 — OUTCOME CLASSIFICATION
====================================================

Classify each pre-open candidate after market data becomes available.

Use outcomes such as:

- STRONG_CONTINUATION
- MODERATE_CONTINUATION
- FLAT
- EARLY_REVERSAL
- LATE_REVERSAL
- FALSE_BREAKOUT
- NO_LIQUIDITY
- DATA_INCOMPLETE
- INVALID_SIGNAL

Define transparent thresholds.

Example:

For bullish pre-open candidates:

STRONG_CONTINUATION:
- price_0930 >= actual_open + 1%
- and max_adverse_excursion within acceptable limit

MODERATE_CONTINUATION:
- price_0930 > actual_open
- but return below strong threshold

EARLY_REVERSAL:
- price moves against pre-open direction within first 15 minutes

Do equivalent logic for bearish candidates.

Do not optimise thresholds using only one trading day.
