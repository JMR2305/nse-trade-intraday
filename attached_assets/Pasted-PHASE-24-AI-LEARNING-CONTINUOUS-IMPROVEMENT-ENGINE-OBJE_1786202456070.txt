PHASE 24 – AI LEARNING & CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT ENGINE

OBJECTIVE

ApexQuant AI should not only execute paper trades.

It should continuously learn from every completed trading session.

The system should identify:

• Why trades succeeded.
• Why trades failed.
• Which strategies perform best.
• Which strategies should be disabled.
• Which Risk rules reject profitable trades.
• Which Risk rules prevent losses.
• Which indicators actually improve accuracy.

This phase creates a complete AI Learning Engine.
