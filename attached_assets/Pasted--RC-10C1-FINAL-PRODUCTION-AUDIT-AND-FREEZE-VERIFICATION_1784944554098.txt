7. CORRECTNESS AUDIT
------------------------------------------------------------

Independently inspect and test the following high-risk cases:

### Recovery

- Snapshot restores all open positions.
- Snapshot restores cash, margin, daily P&L, peak equity, version, and timestamps.
- Replay after snapshot does not duplicate fills.
- A fill_id differing from event idempotency_key does not break recovery.
- Corrupt snapshot is rejected safely.
- Cold-start recovery uses configured initial capital.
- Readiness remains false until recovery and reconciliation complete.

### Cash and reservations

- available + blocked equals total.
- Exact reservation fill.
- Partial fill.
- Slippage above reserved amount.
- Fill below reserved amount.
- Reservation release.
- Duplicate release.
- Failed fill processing rolls back atomically.
- No negative cash unless explicitly permitted.

### Position accounting

- Open.
- Increase.
- Partial reduction.
- Full close.
- FIFO lot accounting.
- Duplicate fill.
- Out-of-order fill.
- Side reversal.
- Short positions, if supported.
- Fees and confirmed-charge adjustments.
- Realised and unrealised P&L.

### Exposure

- Pending-order exposure included.
- Partial fills are not double counted.
- Reserved capital is not double counted.
- Instrument exposure.
- Sector exposure.
- Strategy exposure.
- Long, short, gross, and net exposure.
- Stale market prices.

### Allocation and sizing

- Cash reserve is never allocated.
- Stale allocation decision is rejected.
- Portfolio state-version mismatch is rejected.
- Concurrent allocation requests cannot over-allocate.
- Lot-size rounding never increases risk beyond a limit.
- Quantity zero results in rejection.
- AI confidence cannot independently approve a trade.

### Limits

- Exact-boundary behavior.
- Daily-loss breach.
- Drawdown breach.
- Maximum positions.
- Maximum pending orders.
- Instrument, sector, and strategy limits.
- Critical limit may request the existing RC-8 kill switch.
- No competing kill switch exists.

14. FINDING SEVERITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Classify findings as:

CRITICAL
- Can place an order without RC-8 or RC-7.
- Can activate live trading.
- Can corrupt positions, cash, or P&L without detection.
- Recovery can create duplicate positions or fills.
- Direct broker access bypasses RC-10D.
- Credential exposure.

HIGH
- Incorrect exposure or P&L in realistic scenarios.
- Reconciliation fails to block trading on a Critical mismatch.
- Cash reservation invariant failure.
- Data loss on normal restart.
- Broken migration.
- Concurrency defect causing over-allocation.
- Material regression in a frozen phase.

MEDIUM
- Operational weakness with a safe workaround.
- Missing non-critical test.
- Performance concern outside the order-critical path.
- Documentation inconsistency.

LOW
- Style, maintainability, or minor observability issue.
