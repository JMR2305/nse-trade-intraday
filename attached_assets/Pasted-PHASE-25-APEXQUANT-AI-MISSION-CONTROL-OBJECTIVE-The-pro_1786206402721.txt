OBJECTIVE

The project now contains multiple powerful modules:

• AI Live Trading Command Center
• AI Investigation Center
• Historical Backtesting Engine
• Replay Center
• AI Operations Center
• Portfolio
• Trade History

These currently exist as separate pages.

Phase 25 unifies them into ONE integrated Mission Control.

This becomes the default landing page during market hours.

===============================================================================
MISSION CONTROL ARCHITECTURE
===============================================================================

Mission Control is NOT another backend.

It is a unified dashboard built entirely on the canonical Event Store,
Portfolio Ledger, Replay Snapshot and Learning Engine.

Mission Control MUST NOT calculate any business logic.

Every widget subscribes to the same backend event stream.
