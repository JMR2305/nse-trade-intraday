PHASE 23 – AI LIVE TRADING COMMAND CENTER + HISTORICAL BACKTESTING PLATFORM

OBJECTIVE

This phase is a MAJOR ARCHITECTURE UPGRADE.

The goal is to eliminate the "black box" nature of ApexQuant AI.

Today, the backend performs scanning, strategy selection, risk evaluation, AI decisions and paper execution, but the user cannot actually see what the AI is doing in real time. Different pages also show inconsistent numbers because they are sourcing data independently.

This phase must create ONE SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH and TWO NEW MAJOR FEATURES:

1. AI Live Trading Command Center
2. Historical Backtesting & Investigation Center

===============================================================================
CRITICAL ARCHITECTURE RULE
===============================================================================

NO PAGE may calculate its own numbers.

NO PAGE may maintain its own copy of pipeline state.

NO PAGE may use demo data.

NO PAGE may use synthetic calculations.

Everything must read from ONE backend event stream.

Create ONE canonical Pipeline Event Store.

Every event emitted by the backend must be written once.

Example events:
