PHASE 11 – INSTITUTIONAL RISK ENGINE

Objective:
Transform the trading system from a stock selector into a professional portfolio risk management platform.

Do NOT remove or modify any existing functionality.

==================================================
1. Dynamic Position Sizing
==================================================

For every BUY/WATCH candidate calculate:

• Recommended Quantity
• Capital Allocation %
• Position Value
• Maximum Risk (₹)
• Expected Reward (₹)
• Risk/Reward Ratio

Position sizing should consider:

- confidence
- volatility
- ATR
- stop distance
- account balance
- max portfolio allocation
- existing holdings
