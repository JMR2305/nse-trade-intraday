====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Build an isolated analytics layer that tracks every intraday signal from creation through its subsequent market outcome.

The system must answer:

- Which strategies generate useful signals?
- Which signals continue in the predicted direction?
- Which signals reverse?
- Which signals are rejected by risk controls?
- Which signals become paper trades?
- Which signals are missed?
- Which market regimes produce the best results?
- Does AI agreement improve signal quality?
- Does pre-open context improve prediction quality?
- Which factors contribute most to successful outcomes?
- Where are false positives and false negatives occurring?

Phase 5C must not alter the signal-generation process.

It must observe, record, reconcile and report only.
