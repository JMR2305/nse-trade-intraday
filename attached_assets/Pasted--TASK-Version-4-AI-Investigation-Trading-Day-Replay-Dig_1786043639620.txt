# TASK: Version 4 – AI Investigation & Trading Day Replay (Digital Twin)

This is a NEW FEATURE.

Do NOT modify the existing Trading Timeline page.

Create a completely new page called:

AI Investigation Centre
OR
Trading Day Replay

accessible from the Operations menu.

=========================================================
OBJECTIVE
=========================================================

The purpose is NOT to show logs.

The purpose is to replay an entire trading day exactly as every AI agent experienced it.

The replay must visually animate the complete pipeline.

Users should be able to investigate:

• Why a stock was bought
• Why a stock was rejected
• Which agent rejected it
• Which score caused rejection
• When the BUY happened
• When the SELL happened
• Whether the AI missed an opportunity
• Whether the stop-loss should have changed
• Whether another exit would have been better

Think of it as a "digital twin" of the trading engine.
