OBJECTIVE

Build a comprehensive Advanced Paper Trading Analytics module for ApexQuant AI.

This module analyses all completed paper trades, recommendations, and pre-open validation results to measure trading performance, strategy effectiveness, execution quality, and behavioural patterns.

This module MUST remain completely READ-ONLY and ADVISORY-ONLY.

It MUST NEVER

• Place orders
• Modify paper trades
• Modify strategies
• Modify AI models
• Modify portfolio
• Modify risk parameters
• Modify historical data

It should only analyse, compare and report.
