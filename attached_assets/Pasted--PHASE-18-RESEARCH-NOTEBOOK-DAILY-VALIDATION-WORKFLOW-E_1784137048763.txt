# PHASE 18 — RESEARCH NOTEBOOK, DAILY VALIDATION WORKFLOW & EVIDENCE ACCUMULATION

## OBJECTIVE

The NSE Trader platform is now engineering-complete for research and paper trading.

This phase must not add new trading strategies, indicators, scoring logic, broker execution, or major dashboards.

The goal is to create a disciplined daily research workflow that records what the platform saw, what it recommended, what the user did, what happened afterward, and what was learned.

This phase is strictly:

- Research only
- Paper trading only
- No automatic live orders
- No automatic model promotion
- No automatic rule changes
- Human review required

======================================================================

## 1. CREATE A RESEARCH NOTEBOOK

Add a new sidebar page named:

Research Notebook

The page should act as a permanent daily research journal.

Each trading day should have one notebook entry containing:

- Trading date
- Market open and close status
- Market regime
- NIFTY trend
- BANK NIFTY trend
- India VIX
- Market breadth
- Strongest sectors
- Weakest sectors
- Latest scan ID
- Scan timestamp
- Data provider
- Data-quality summary
- Best opportunities
- Stocks to avoid
- Open paper positions
- New paper trades
- Closed paper trades
- Trades skipped
- Risk warnings
- Validation results
- System-health summary
- User notes
- Lessons learned
- Follow-up actions

Every notebook entry must be linked to the exact canonical scan snapshot used that day.

## 2. AUTOMATIC DAILY ENTRY CREATION

Automatically create one draft notebook entry after the first successful scan of each trading day.

Update the same entry throughout the day instead of creating duplicates.

At market close, finalize the notebook entry with:

- End-of-day portfolio value
- Daily paper P&L
- Trades opened
- Trades closed
- Stops hit
- Targets hit
- Exit recommendations
- Missed opportunities
- False-positive signals
- Data-quality issues
- Alerts generated
- Validation warnings
- QA status
- Final daily summary

Use Asia/Kolkata timezone.

Do not create duplicate entries for the same trading date.

## 5. DAILY VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Add a daily checklist inside each notebook entry:

Before market:

- Data provider healthy
- Market calendar valid
- Latest historical data available
- Canonical scan context available
- Paper-trading mode confirmed
- Live execution disabled
- Kill switch available
- No critical QA failures

During market:

- Scan completed successfully
- No stale-data violations
- No cross-page mismatches
- No duplicate paper trades
- No duplicate alerts
- Open positions have stop and target
- Risk limits respected

After market:

- Daily notebook finalized
- Paper trades reconciled
- Daily P&L reconciled
- Validation report generated
- Errors reviewed
- Alerts reviewed
- Lessons recorded

Show PASS, FAIL, WARNING, or NOT APPLICABLE for each item.

## 14. AUTOMATION SAFETY

The notebook may automatically:

- Create draft entries
- Attach scans
- Attach paper trades
- Attach validation results
- Generate summaries
- Generate reports

The notebook must never automatically:

- Place trades
- Close trades
- Change position size
- Change confidence thresholds
- Promote models
- Enable strategies
- Disable strategies
- Change risk limits
- Connect live execution

Human approval remains mandatory for all trading or model changes.

## 17. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Phase 18 is complete only when:

1. A daily notebook entry is created after the first successful scan.
2. The entry updates throughout the same trading day.
3. The day can be finalized after market close.
4. Every entry links to the canonical scan.
5. Paper trades and outcomes are linked correctly.
6. User notes can be saved and searched.
7. Daily, weekly, and monthly reviews are generated.
8. Evidence accumulation is visible.
9. Issue tracking works.
10. Exports work.
11. No trading logic changes occur.
12. No real orders can be placed.
13. All Phase 17 QA checks still pass.
