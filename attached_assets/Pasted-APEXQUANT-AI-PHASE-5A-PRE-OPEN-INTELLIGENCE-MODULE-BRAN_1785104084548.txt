====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Build a Pre-Open Intelligence module that captures and analyses NSE pre-open market information between 09:00 and 09:15 IST.

The module must identify:

- gap-up stocks
- gap-down stocks
- buy/sell order imbalance
- indicative opening prices
- final opening prices
- executed pre-open quantities
- unusual pre-open activity
- sector strength
- high-priority intraday watchlist candidates

Pre-open data must NOT independently generate or execute a trade.

It may only:

- rank stocks
- create watchlist candidates
- adjust advisory confidence
- provide context to the post-09:15 strategy engine

====================================================
PART 3 — PRE-OPEN SCHEDULER
====================================================

Create an IST-aware scheduler.

Recommended operation:

08:45
- initialise module
- validate provider and database
- load previous-close reference data

08:55
- run readiness check

09:00–09:08
- collect pre-open snapshots every 30 seconds

09:08–09:12
- collect snapshots every 15 seconds where supported

09:12–09:15
- capture final auction/opening state

09:15
- freeze final pre-open snapshot
- generate ranked intraday watchlist

09:20
- reconcile indicative prices against actual opening prices

Do not collect pre-open data outside the applicable session except for replay or testing.

Use the existing NSE market calendar and IST session logic.

====================================================
PART 4 — ANALYTICS
====================================================

Calculate:

1. Gap percentage

gap_percent =
(final_or_indicative_open - previous_close)
÷ previous_close × 100

2. Buy/sell imbalance

imbalance =
total_buy_quantity - total_sell_quantity

3. Imbalance percentage

imbalance_percent =
(total_buy_quantity - total_sell_quantity)
÷ max(total_buy_quantity + total_sell_quantity, 1)
× 100

4. Participation score

Use matched quantity or executed quantity compared with:

- the rest of the pre-open universe
- recent average volume where available
- symbol liquidity profile

5. Pre-open opportunity score

Create a transparent deterministic score from 0–100 using:

- gap strength
- order imbalance
- executed quantity
- liquidity
- sector confirmation
- index direction
- data freshness
- volatility risk

Do not use opaque AI scoring for the primary ranking.

Store individual factor contributions.

====================================================
PART 7 — POST-OPEN CONFIRMATION
====================================================

Pre-open candidates must pass a separate post-open confirmation stage.

No paper entry before the configured strategy permits it.

Confirm using:

- actual opening price
- first 5-minute or configured candle
- live volume
- relative volume
- VWAP relationship
- opening-range breakout
- sector strength
- NIFTY / Bank NIFTY context
- India VIX context
- spread and liquidity
- stale-data gate
- risk-engine approval

If pre-open conditions do not confirm after market open:

- downgrade to WATCH
- or mark NO_TRADE
